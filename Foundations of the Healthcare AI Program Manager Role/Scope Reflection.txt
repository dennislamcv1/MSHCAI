In the Organizational Goals component, and again at Milestone 3.3 of the Rollout Outline, I flagged that the CIO Email describes the step after the pilot as "a broader evaluation" while the CMO Email refers to what is needed "before broader adoption is considered," and I marked it "Note: inputs disagree—for senior lead confirmation" rather than choosing one. Picking either wording would have meant deciding what the executive committee is actually being asked to commit to after the pilot, which is a scope decision that belongs to the two executives and my Senior AI Program Manager, not to a coordinator 20 days into the role. My job at this stage is to organize what the discovery inputs say and make the open questions easy to find, so I quoted both sources, kept Phase 3 conditional on a documented leadership decision, and listed the item as A1 in the confirmation list at the end. Flagging it also protects the briefing's credibility: if I had quietly written "adoption," the CIO could reasonably read the document as overstating what was approved. Surfacing the difference now lets my senior lead settle it in one conversation before the executive review instead of having it come up in the room.
